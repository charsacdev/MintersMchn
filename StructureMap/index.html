● green = swing high, ● red = swing low. A swing only counts once price has pulled back by the swing size — a plain percentage of price. Each panel has its own base (15m 1.00% · 5m 0.75% · 1m 0.50%) because a week needs a bigger swing than a day; the slider scales all three together, and each chart header shows what it works out to in points.  ·  The trend is the last two labels: HH + HL = bullish · LH + LL = bearish · HH + LL = expanding · LH + HL = contracting. The zigzag and the ribbon under each chart are coloured by that reading, so you can see the state the whole way along.  ·  A reversal attempt starts the moment a directional trend stops being directional. It can only end two ways: it reaches the opposite direction (SWITCH, solid line) or it falls back into the trend it came from (FAILED, dashed gold).  ·  The reading can never jump straight from bullish to bearish — each swing updates only one half of the pair — so there is always a mixed step in between. That step is the attempt, and the Route column names which one it went through.  ·  Completed in the summary is switches ÷ all attempts: how often a reversal that started actually finished.
