Swing maps — the two swings that set each reading are drawn larger; dashed lines are the prices that produce the next HH or LL
Everything here is the raw tick path. No candles, no OHLC — the line is the actual sequence of quotes.  ·  ● green = swing high, ● red = swing low. A swing only counts once price has retraced by the swing filter, which scales as σ√n so the three panels stay comparable.  ·  The trend is the last two labels, nothing else: HH + HL = bullish · LH + LL = bearish · HH + LL = expanding, no trend · LH + HL = contracting, no trend.  ·  The two mixed readings are the reason for reading a pair rather than one number — they let the map say there is no trend instead of being forced to pick a side.  ·  Price beyond the last swing shows as forming: it is on its way to a new label but is not counted until it retraces, because until then it is not a swing.  ·  1 u = —.
